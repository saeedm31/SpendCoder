How it works

Four steps, built on your own history

SpendCoder learns from lines your team has already coded, so it follows your categories, not a generic list.

1You

Share past coded lines

An export from your ERP or AP system: the line description and the category it was booked to.

2We

Train it on your categories

A language model that already understands business English learns your category list from those examples.

3SpendCoder

Code new lines

Confident lines are coded automatically. Unsure lines go to a review queue with the top 3 suggestions.

4Your team

Correct and improve

Every correction becomes a new example. Regular updates go live only when they test better than the current version.

You stay in control

Automation where it is safe, people where it matters

You set the confidence bar

Choose how sure SpendCoder must be before it codes on its own. A higher bar means fewer mistakes and more lines for review.

A person checks the unsure lines

Low-confidence lines never post on their own. Reviewers see the top 3 suggestions and pick one in a click.

Your data stays with you

It runs on your own cloud account or servers. Lines are not sent to outside AI services.

Every decision is traceable

Each suggestion, confidence score and human decision can be logged for audit, and each model version is kept.
